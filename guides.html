Ul Arena

Located on the North Campus near East Gate, the UL Sport Arena is Ireland's premier university sports complex. It houses a 50-metre Olympic swimming pool, an indoor sprint track, multi-purpose sports courts, and a comprehensive student gym. Bring your UL student ID for discounted membership rates, and aim to train before 4:30 PM to beat the evening rush.

Stables

Opened in 1987 in the heart of the Student Centre Courtyard, The Stables Club is UL's iconic campus pub and social hub. Known for its spacious outdoor courtyard, live music, and weekly student nights, it is the main meeting spot after lectures. Drop by on Tuesdays between 12:00 PM and 5:30 PM to browse the weekly Student Farmers' Market right outside.

Shannon River/ Bridge

Spanning 350 metres across the River Shannon, the Living Bridge connects UL's South Campus in County Limerick with the North Campus in County Clare. As the longest pedestrian bridge in Ireland, its gentle curving design offers scenic views of the river and surrounding woodland—making it the essential walking route between the Main Building and the Arena.

Maguires Pitch

Situated on the North Campus just across the Living Bridge, Maguire's Pitches are UL's expansive all-weather and grass sports fields used for GAA, soccer, rugby, and Ultimate Frisbee. Whether you are competing in intervarsity matches, joining an evening club training session, or watching a floodlit game, these pitches are the outdoor heart of campus sport.

Pavilion

Overlooking Maguire's Pitches and the River Shannon on the North Campus, The Pavilion is a bright, spacious campus restaurant and bar. Serving hot lunches, carvery dinners, and post-training refreshments seven days a week, it is a great spot to unwind after a match or enjoy a quieter meal away from the busy Main Building cafeterias.

Forest Path

Winding along the banks of the River Shannon between Plassey House and the Boathouse, the Forest Path is a peaceful, tree-lined trail right on the edge of campus. Popular with runners, walkers, and cyclists, this scenic riverside walk links directly toward Limerick city and offers a quiet escape into nature between study sessions in the library.
